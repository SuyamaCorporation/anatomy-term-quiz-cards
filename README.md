# Anatomy Term Flashcard Quiz

Study 36 anatomical terms across skeletal, muscular, and organ systems with flipping flashcards and a type-in quiz mode.

## Features
- 36 built-in anatomy cards: 13 skeletal, 11 muscular, 12 organ terms — each with a full definition
- System filter buttons to study one body system at a time
- Study Mode: flip cards with a 3D animation to reveal the definition; tracks how many cards studied
- Quiz Mode: definition is shown first; type the term and check your answer with fuzzy matching
- Missed Card review deck automatically populated from incorrect quiz answers

## Usage
Select a body system filter or keep All. Choose Study Mode to flip through cards at your own pace. Switch to Quiz Mode to type in the term from memory. Click Check or press Enter to see if you were right. Incorrect answers are added to the Missed deck for focused review. Progress bar shows your position in the current deck.
